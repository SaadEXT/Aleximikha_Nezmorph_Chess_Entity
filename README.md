Aleximikha – A Human-Style Chess Engine & AI Chess Ecosystem

Aleximikha is not just a chess engine — she is a personality-driven, human-style tactical artist fused with a full chess training ecosystem.

Designed as a metaphorical fusion of Morphy, Tal, Nezhmetdinov, and Alekhine, Aleximikha plays creative, aggressive, and initiative-driven chess rather than cold, purely materialistic engine moves. Her playstyle prioritizes dynamic imbalances, sacrifices, attacking potential, and poetic combinations — bringing back the romance of classical attacking chess.

But Aleximikha is more than an opponent.

She is an interactive AI entity powered by a local LLM that can chat mid-game on any topic, explain her reasoning behind moves, and adapt her tone and personality dynamically. Her vivid character is reflected through language, accent, and expressive commentary.

The project includes multiple integrated modes:

Play Mode – Engage in human-like, artistic chess while conversing naturally with the engine.

Tutor Mode – Learn while playing. After each match, Aleximikha performs a future-focused 3×3 analysis:

3 decisive swing moments

3 critical “why” questions per moment

Pattern recognition of the user’s recurring weaknesses
Based on this, she recommends targeted puzzles.

Analysis Mode – Upload PGN or input a position for evaluation and suggested continuations.

Coach Mode (In Development) – A long-term AI coaching system designed to simulate structured chess training, including book-based courses, weakness tracking, skill assessment, and personalized study plans.

Additional features include:

Puzzle library and recommendation engine

Game archive system

Dedicated chat mode

Planned aesthetic, fully polished GUI for non-technical users

Aleximikha aims to evolve into a complete chess operating system — a creative rival, a tutor, an analyst, and a long-term coach — all wrapped inside a unified and personality-rich experience.
